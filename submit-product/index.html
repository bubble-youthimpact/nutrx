About Us

Our Story

We created this organization because, as a team, we kept seeing the same heartbreaking pattern ripple through our communities — seniors confused by exaggerated ads, families harmed by unsafe supplements, and ordinary consumers wasting money on products that never worked. Each of us had witnessed someone we love fall victim to false health claims, and we realized the issue wasn’t personal — it was societal. There was no single, trustworthy source to show what’s verified, what’s fake, or what truly fits each person’s body.

So we built Nutrx with one clear vision: a world where every health claim earns our trust.

A Better Way to Find What Fits

We built Nutrx to be the most efficient way to find the right health-functional products. No more scrolling through endless fine print or falling for flashy commercials.

Our Team

JK

Joan Kim

Founder & CEO

Today, the health market is flooded with endless choices, promises, and unfortunately, widespread misinformation. As a consumer, trying to differentiate between a truly beneficial functional product and a fraudulent one can feel overwhelming and sometimes even dangerous. I founded NUTRX because I believe that transparency shouldn’t be a luxury; it should be a basic right. Driven by a passion for consumer safety, I set out to bridge the gap between complex regulatory data and the public, creating a platform that replaces guesswork with verified truth. Ultimately, NUTRX was born out of the necessity to empower individuals with factual information, transforming the way we navigate and approach dietary supplements and functional foods.

This Privacy Policy explains how (“Nutrx”, “we”, “us”) collects, uses, stores and shares information when you use the Nutrx website (the “Service”). Nutrx helps people compare health-functional products and learn which ones are verified, recalled or deceptively marketed.

1. Summary

  • You can browse Nutrx without an account. We don’t run ads or advertising trackers.
  • If you create an account, we receive your name and email address — from Google if you choose “Continue with Google” (plus your profile photo), or from the sign-up form if you use an email and password. We use them to create your account and show who is signed in.
  • We store what you choose to save — favorite products and optional profile details — so you can see them across devices.
  • We do not sell your information, and we do not use Google user data for advertising.
  • You can delete your account and saved data at any time from your profile page.

3. How we access, use, store and share Google user data

What we access. When you sign in with Google, we request only the basic sign-in permissions (openid, email and profile). This gives us your name, email address and profile photo URL. We do not request access to your Gmail, Google Drive, Contacts, Calendar or any other Google data.

How we use it. We use this information only to sign you in, create and identify your Nutrx account, display your name and photo in the site header and on your profile page, and link your favorites and profile details to your account.

How we store it. Your account (whether you sign in with Google or with an email and password) is managed by Google Firebase Authentication. Your name, email, photo URL, favorites and profile details are stored in a Google Cloud Firestore database that only your signed-in account can read or change.

How we share it. We do not sell, rent or trade Google user data. We do not share it with third parties except (a) the service providers that run the Service on our behalf (Google Firebase), (b) when you ask us to, or (c) when required by law, to protect against fraud or abuse, or as part of a merger or acquisition with notice to you. We do not use Google user data for advertising, and we do not use it to train AI or machine-learning models. People at Nutrx do not read your data unless you ask us to (for example, for support), it is needed for security, or the law requires it.

Nutrx’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. How we use information

  • To provide the Service: sign-in, favorites, comparisons and your profile.
  • To respond to messages and product review submissions.
  • To keep the Service secure and prevent abuse.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

We don’t use your information for advertising, and we don’t build advertising profiles.

5. Sharing & service providers

We use a small number of providers to run Nutrx. They process information only to provide their service to us:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore): account sign-in (Google or email and password), account emails such as verification and password-reset links, and storage of your saved data.
  • FormSubmit (formsubmit.co): delivers contact messages and product review submissions, including any photo you attach, to our email inbox.
  • Google Fonts: delivers the site’s typeface. Your browser contacts Google’s servers to load it.
  • Our website host: serves the site’s pages.

Product pages link to outside websites (for example FDA notices, articles, videos and stores). Those sites have their own privacy policies.

6. Storage, cookies & local storage

Nutrx does not use advertising or analytics cookies. If you sign in, Firebase stores a sign-in token in your browser (in local storage or IndexedDB) to keep you signed in. If you use a local profile instead of Google sign-in, your profile and favorites are saved only in your browser’s local storage on that device and are never sent to us.

7. Retention & deletion

We keep your account information and saved data until you delete your account. To delete it, sign in, open your profile page and choose Delete account. This permanently removes your profile, favorites and sign-in record from our systems. Backups maintained by our providers are cleared on their normal schedule. You can also ask us to delete your data by contacting us.

You can remove Nutrx’s access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections. Messages and product submissions arrive in our email inbox and are kept only as long as needed to respond to them and review the product.

8. Your choices & rights

You can view and edit your profile details, remove favorites, and delete your account from your profile page at any time. Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your personal information, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to withdraw consent. To make a request, contact us. We will respond within the time required by applicable law.

9. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Access to saved data is restricted so that each signed-in account can read and change only its own record. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your information.

10. Children

Nutrx is not directed to children under . We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under without the consent of a parent or guardian. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

11. International transfers

Our service providers may process information in countries other than yours, including the United States. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards for these transfers.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will change the “Last updated” date above, and if the changes are significant we will provide a more prominent notice. We will not use Google user data in new ways without telling you first and, where required, asking for your consent.
